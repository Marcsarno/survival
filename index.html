Sarno Survive

The power's gone and South Florida is under snow. Leave the pavilion shelter, find supplies and firewood, bring them back, and keep the fire going.

Keyboard & mouse

  • WASD / arrows move · Shift sprint
  • E interact / gather / search (hold)
  • Right mouse aim (hold to steady) · Left click shoot
  • F axe swing / shove
  • L lantern · 1–4 use food / medkit / water / oil
  • Wheel or + / − camera height · Z / X rotate camera
  • C low cinematic camera · N skip to night/day
  • M map · H help · Esc pause

Touch

  • Left thumb: joystick
  • E interact (hold to gather)
  • 🎯 hold to aim, release to fire
  • 🪓 swing · 🏮 lantern · 🏃 sprint
  • ＋/－ camera height · tap items to use

Survival

  • Warmth drains outside — faster at night. Stand by fire to recover.
  • Carried items are lost if you fall. Store them at the shelter stash.
  • The lantern lights your way — and lets the frozen see you.
