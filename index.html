Sarno Survive

Opening test. Marc is searching for his daughter, Arianna. Lines are text only for now.

Keyboard

  • WASD / arrows walk (W is up-screen)
  • E interact (pick up, open, climb)
  • Esc pause · R restart

Touch

  • Put your thumb down anywhere on the left side and drag to walk
  • E interact

Test build. Stand-in character and graybox scenery. Marc: Tripo model. Textures and props: Poly Haven (CC0). Vehicles and boat: Quaternius, Kay Lousberg (CC0), Poly by Google and others (CC BY). Trees, buildings and props built in Blender. See CREDITS.md.
